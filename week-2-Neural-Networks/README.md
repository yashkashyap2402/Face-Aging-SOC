# Week 2 - Neural Networks & Deep Learning

## Overview

This week focused on understanding the fundamentals of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) and getting started with two popular deep learning frameworks: PyTorch and TensorFlow. The objective was to learn how neural networks are built, trained, and optimized before moving on to computer vision tasks.

## Topics Covered

### Neural Networks

* Introduction to Artificial Neural Networks
* Perceptron and Neurons
* Forward Propagation
* Backpropagation
* Gradient Descent
* Activation Functions
* Loss Functions
* Optimizers

### PyTorch

* Tensor Creation
* Tensor Operations
* Tensor Reshaping
* Autograd
* Neural Network Construction
* Loss Functions
* Optimizers
* Training Loop
* Saving and Loading Models

### TensorFlow

* TensorFlow Basics
* Tensor Creation
* Variables
* Tensor Operations
* Building Neural Networks with Keras
* Model Compilation
* Model Training
* Model Evaluation
* Saving and Loading Models

## Files Included

* `pytorch_basics.ipynb` – Practice notebook covering PyTorch fundamentals.
* `tensorflow_basics.ipynb` – Practice notebook covering TensorFlow fundamentals.

## Learning Outcome

By the end of this week, I gained a strong understanding of the basic concepts of deep learning and became familiar with PyTorch and TensorFlow for building, training, and evaluating simple neural network models.
